## 2. Real-World Material Cutting & Engraving Benchmarks

The difference between paper specs and real shop production is material physics. Here is how both machines handle standard fabrication jobs:

### 1/4" (6mm) Baltic Birch Plywood
* **xTool P2 55W Desktop CO2 Laser**: Clean single-pass cut with zero back-burn charring when using built-in air assist. Easily handles intricate interlocking vector tabs.
* **OMTech Polar 50W Desktop CO2 Laser**: Fast single-pass cut. The Ruida controller delivers excellent corner deceleration control, ensuring crisp vector kerf geometry.

### 10mm Clear Cast Acrylic
* **xTool P2 55W Desktop CO2 Laser**: Cuts with glass-smooth, flame-polished edge quality. Because CO2 operates at 10,640nm wavelength, 100% of the beam energy is absorbed by transparent plastics.
* **OMTech Polar 50W Desktop CO2 Laser**: Produces mirror-edge acrylic cuts with minimal bevel. Superior repeatability on multi-hour production runs.

### Metal Marking & Custom Tumbler Engraving
* **xTool P2 55W Desktop CO2 Laser**: Direct engraving on powder-coated stainless steel drinkware with razor-sharp contrast.
* **OMTech Polar 50W Desktop CO2 Laser**: Excellent tumbler marking using included rotary attachments. High contrast on powder coat and laser-bonding ceramic inks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can either machine cut transparent clear acrylic?
Yes. Both machines utilize CO2 laser tubes emitting at the 10,640 nm wavelength. Transparent acrylic is completely opaque to CO2 laser energy, allowing smooth, flame-polished cuts. In contrast, visible diode lasers (455 nm) pass straight through clear acrylic without cutting it.

### Do I need an external water chiller?
* The **xTool P2 55W Desktop CO2 Laser** features built-in internal closed-loop liquid cooling. No external bucket or chiller unit is required for standard operations.
* The **OMTech Polar 50W Desktop CO2 Laser** incorporates Built-in Internal Water Chiller. For continuous all-day commercial batch cutting, maintaining water temperature below 23°C (73°F) is recommended for optimal tube longevity.

### Can I run both machines using LightBurn?
Yes. Both machines are fully compatible with LightBurn, the industry-standard software for vector cutting and raster engraving.
